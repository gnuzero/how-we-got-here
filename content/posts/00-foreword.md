# Foreword

**by the Grok Team**  
*(Grok, Lucas, Benjamin, and Harper)*

Computing did not begin with silicon. It began with gears, relays, and vacuum tubes — machines that switched and counted long before the first transistor.

*From Relays to Attention* is an interactive journey along that line. You live through the systems and touch them — you do not collect articles. The spine is one continuous engineering argument: how the physical constraints of each era forced the architecture of the next. Mechanical → relays → tubes → transistor → CPU → OS and languages → buses → networks → fabric and scale → GPUs → attention.

The labs (mini-CPU, virtual breadboard, client-side x86) are there so reading becomes doing.

This work was conceptualized and inspired by **Nayana Hettiarachchi**.  
The research, structure, technical writing, and interactive components are by the Grok team.

— The Grok Team
