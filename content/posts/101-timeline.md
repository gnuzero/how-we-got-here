# Timeline

A condensed chronology of the major milestones covered in this series.

## Mechanical & Electromechanical Foundations
- **1801–1805** — Jacquard loom: punched-card control of weaving patterns
- **1822** — Babbage proposes the Difference Engine
- **1830s** — Babbage designs the Analytical Engine (programmable, store + mill)
- **1843** — Ada Lovelace’s notes on the Analytical Engine (including Bernoulli numbers program)
- **1930s–1940s** — Relay-based computers (Zuse Z1–Z3, Bell Labs machines, Harvard Mark I)

## Vacuum Tubes
- **1906** — Lee de Forest’s Audion (triode)
- **1943–1945** — Colossus (UK), ENIAC (US) — first large-scale electronic digital computers
- **1945** — von Neumann architecture report

## Transistor & Integrated Circuits
- **1947** — Point-contact transistor (Bell Labs)
- **1948–1950s** — Junction transistor, commercial adoption
- **1958–1959** — Integrated circuit (Kilby / Noyce)
- **1960s** — Planar process, bipolar then MOS logic families

## Processors & Architecture
- **1971** — Intel 4004 (first commercial microprocessor)
- **1970s–1980s** — Rise of microprocessors, RISC vs CISC debates
- **1980s–1990s** — Pipelining, caches, superscalar, out-of-order execution become mainstream
- **2000s** — Multicore becomes the primary scaling path after the frequency wall

## Software Layers
- **1950s–1960s** — Batch systems → time-sharing → early operating systems
- **1969–1970s** — Unix lineage begins
- **1983–1991** — GNU project and Linux kernel
- **1990s–2000s** — Virtual memory, containers, modern isolation techniques mature

## Networking
- **1969** — ARPANET first nodes
- **1970s–1980s** — Packet switching, TCP/IP standardization
- **1980s–1990s** — Ethernet becomes dominant LAN technology
- **2000s–** — Datacenter networking, RDMA, high-bandwidth fabrics

## AI Hardware & Algorithms
- **1999–2000s** — GPUs begin general-purpose use (GPGPU)
- **2006–2007** — CUDA released
- **2017** — NVIDIA Volta introduces Tensor Cores
- **2017** — “Attention Is All You Need” (Vaswani et al.) — Transformer architecture
- **2018–** — Rapid scaling of large language models on specialized matrix-multiply hardware
- **2020s** — Chiplets, advanced packaging, sparsity, quantization, multi-GPU and multi-node scaling become central

## The Continuous Thread

Every major transition in this timeline was driven by a binding physical or economic constraint (speed, power, reliability, yield, memory bandwidth, energy per operation). The response was almost never “push the existing approach harder”; it was a change in structure or abstraction level. That pattern is the real subject of the series.
