Picture a clerk at a desk. Marks on paper. A written list of steps: add this, carry that, write the total here. When the list is followed carefully, the result matches. When the clerk is tired, a step gets skipped. When the clerk is unsure, a guess creeps in.

The brass engines we meet next do the same three jobs the careful clerk does — hold marks, follow a list of steps, write a result — and they do not tire, skip, or guess.
