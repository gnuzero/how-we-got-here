A caravan crosses a desert because people agreed where to stop and what the marks on clay or cloth mean. The camels walk. The clever part is the agreement: which notch means water, which fold means a day’s rest, which seal means the load is paid. The caravan itself is not inventing the map.

The engines ahead are like that: the marks and the stops carry the cleverness. The moving parts only follow them.
