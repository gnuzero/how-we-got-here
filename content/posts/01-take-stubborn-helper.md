Imagine a helper who never gets bored, never guesses what you “really” meant, and never improves the request into something nicer. You ask for forty turns of the crank; you get forty. You ask for the list as written; you get the list as written. Stubborn is a virtue when the chore must not drift.

The brass engines are that kind of helper: tireless, literal, and unwilling to rewrite the ask.
