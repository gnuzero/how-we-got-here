A player-piano roll is a strip of paper with holes. Air passes where the holes are; hammers strike. Run the same roll twice and you hear the same piece. The piano does not remember last Tuesday’s mood — only the holes passing under the tracker bar.

That repeatability — same marks, same motion, same result — is what the brass engines are for.
