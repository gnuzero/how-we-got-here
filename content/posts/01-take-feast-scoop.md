At a wedding feast a scoop fills each plate. The scoop is the rule: one full measure, then the next guest. A fair host does not dig deeper for the tenth plate because the pot looks low or the guest looks hungry. The measure stays the measure.

That fairness — same action, same size, no special case invented on the spot — is the habit the brass engines keep when they add and carry.
