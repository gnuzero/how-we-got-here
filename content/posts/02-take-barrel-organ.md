A barrel organ does not know a hymn. Pegs on a barrel lift levers; air reaches pipes; a tune comes out. Change the pegs, change the song. The box has no opinion about the music — only the arrangement of pegs.

The engines ahead work the same way: the arrangement you give them is what they play. They do not hum along with a better idea of their own.
