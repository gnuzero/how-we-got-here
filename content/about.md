# About This Journey

**How We Got There** is an interactive journey along the engineering lineage of digital computing — from gears and relays through tubes, transistors, processors, operating systems, networks, GPUs, and the attention mechanisms behind large language models.

It is not an encyclopedia. The spine is constraint → architecture. Experience and labs come first; references are doors out where a topic already has strong primary sources.

Optional widen-the-lens beats sit beside the main path. Thin indexes: [Optional Learning Paths](/paths/) — indexes, not a parallel book.

## Interactive Labs

Three client-side labs are included:

- **Mini-CPU** — A fully self-contained 8-bit toy ISA with assembler and step/run emulator. Works offline.
- **Breadboard** — Virtual circuit simulator (Falstad CircuitJS) for building logic gates and small digital circuits.
- **VM Terminal** — Full x86 Linux environment running in the browser via WebAssembly (v86).
