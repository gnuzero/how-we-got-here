Virtual Breadboard Lab

Interactive circuit simulator — build logic gates, CMOS stages, and small digital circuits directly in-browser
Open in new tab ↗ Wokwi (physical breadboard + real MCU) ↗
This embeds Falstad's CircuitJS — free, no account required, runs entirely client-side. Good for logic gates, CMOS inverters/NAND/NOR, and small combinational circuits (matches Phase 2 & Phase 3 posts).

For posts that need an actual physical breadboard layout with real component placement (resistors, LEDs, a real microcontroller like an ATmega328/ESP32) rather than a schematic, use the Wokwi link instead — it requires a free account to save projects but the simulator itself is free to use.

To embed a specific saved circuit instead of a blank canvas, replace the iframe src with a CircuitJS permalink (Falstad's "share" button generates a ?ctz=... URL) or a Wokwi embed/<project-id> URL.
