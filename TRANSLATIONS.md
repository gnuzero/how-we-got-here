## Short Series Description

**English**  
An educational series tracing the complete arc of digital computing — from electromechanical relays and vacuum tubes through modern GPUs, tensor cores, and the hardware that makes large-scale attention mechanisms possible. 63 technical chapters + Foreword + Glossary + interactive labs.
